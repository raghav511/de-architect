# iPhone Safari pass: reel icons, mic refusal, coach button, end card
- Reel icons show as symbols instead of words like lock or check (843 reels were affected).
- On iPhone, saying no to speech input now explains why and where to turn it on, instead of saying nothing.
- On phones the coach button no longer covers the simulator's Reset button or the live mock's Start.
- Tapping a finished reel no longer reshuffles its quick check's answers under your finger.
