# The Meridian series: Previously on, Tomorrow on, and the story (ENGAGE 1 and 3)
- Your plan is now a series: Marcus Bell, Meridian's new staff data architect, lives through five seasons that follow your modules, from his first week to his own staff interview loop.
- From the second day, Today opens with Previously on Meridian: a short scene and your recall set before the plan (skip it any time).
- When the day's set is done, it ends on Tomorrow on Meridian with one hook and a one-tap plan for tomorrow that you can add to your calendar. Nothing plays next.
- Meridian series in the Learn menu shows the seasons, the scenes so far and the cast; Profile › Look › Meridian story turns the story text off.
