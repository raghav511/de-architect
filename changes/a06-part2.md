# Module A6 part 2: choosing the approach and the model, guardrails and governance
- AI 6 gains two sections: when to use a better prompt, retrieval, fine-tuning or distillation (with LoRA in one paragraph and fine-tuning data treated as a pipeline), how to choose a model, and guardrails in layers with red teaming as a test suite.
- It also explains the EU AI Act, the NIST AI Risk Management Framework and ISO/IEC 42001 in a paragraph each, for the 'how would you get this past legal' question.
- Four new exercises run in your browser with stand-in models: a fine-tuning dataset with a leak-proof split, an output guard, permission-aware retrieval and a red-team run with a canary secret.
