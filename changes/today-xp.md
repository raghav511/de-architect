# Daily set scored in XP, no daily time limit (F9)
- Today now holds one item from every section of Learn, Practice and Interview that has work: a lesson, reels, coding, design, the simulator, a live question, recall, a loop drill and more. There is no time limit: every row, tab and the day show their minutes as a guide, and XP decides the day and when the module moves on.
- Finish the whole set for +20 XP and a streak day (the weekly freeze is kept). Once a tab is done, One more adds the next item of a section.
- The live mock is one question on weekdays, the full five-question mock on Saturday and the seven-question boss round on Sunday. Anything unfinished carries to the next day, at most one item per section.
- On days without a design or project step, Practice has a 15-minute comparison of two or three components. Profile › Plan has a Daily set choice (smallest or double); your old minute settings are kept.
