# Whole program in Today: Learn, Practice and Interview tabs (F2, F8)
- Today's plan now has three tabs named like the app's main groups, Learn, Practice and Interview, each with its minutes: reading, quizzes and reels; Git, drills, coding, the design problem, project sessions and a simulator challenge; and your module's interview set, the live or written mock, a recall set, the interview loop drill, company questions and coaching.
- Design problems and project sessions now have their own daily minutes (Profile › Plan › Design and build) instead of using your reading time, so the program finishes sooner.
- The interview loop drill is now part of the day, so interview practice gets 10 more minutes by default (40 on weekdays, 55 at weekends); minutes you set yourself are kept.
