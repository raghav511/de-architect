# Reels player: Instagram-style layout, speeds 0.25x to 2x, a real loading state
- Reels now look like Instagram Reels everywhere: the picture fills the screen, the title and caption sit at the bottom left, Save, Speed and Voice are icons on the right, and a round play button shows when a reel is paused.
- Speed now offers 0.25x, 0.5x, 0.75x, Normal, 1.25x, 1.5x, 1.75x and 2x.
- Opening Reels shows the player straight away with a short 'Getting your reels ready' state instead of a bare 'Loading reels…' line.
